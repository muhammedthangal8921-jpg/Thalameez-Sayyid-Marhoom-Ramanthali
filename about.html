Melaad Fest 2026

About the Fest

Fasthabiqul Khairath is the campaign identity for the Thalameez Sayyid Marhoom Ramanthali Melaad Fest 2026 competition.

Fasthabiqul Khairath

The fest brings together two teams—YARMUQIYYA and QADISIYYA—across Senior and Junior Stage and Non-Stage programmes. This website provides a transparent live scoreboard, participant mark breakdowns, overall champions, and an event gallery.

The visual identity uses a professional Islamic-inspired design with restrained geometric patterns, deep green tones, and gold accents.

Competition Structure

  • Senior Stage programmes: 7
  • Senior Non-Stage programmes: 8
  • Junior Stage programmes: 10
  • Junior Non-Stage programmes: 11
  • Two teams with separate Senior, Junior, and Overall totals
